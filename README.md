# Hi, I'm building Joklad CareConnect CRM 👋

Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I'm developing digital solutions to support the delivery and management of compassionate, person-centred home care through **Joklad Healthcare Limited**, based in Glasgow, Scotland.

## 💜 About Joklad Healthcare

Joklad Healthcare Limited provides home care and support services focused on dignity, independence, safety and positive outcomes.

**Our slogan:** Bringing Care to Your Doorstep

🌐 Website: https://www.jokladhealthcare.co.uk

## 🚀 Current Project: Joklad CareConnect CRM

I'm working towards a web-based CRM to help organise our care service operations.

The planned modules include:

- 📩 Enquiries and referrals
- 🏡 Service-user records
- 👥 Staff management
- ✅ Compliance and task tracking

## 🎯 Our Goal

To develop a practical, secure and easy-to-use system that supports efficient administration and high-quality care.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

Care information is sensitive. The CRM will need appropriate access controls, secure authentication, data protection measures and reliable backups before being used with real service-user or staff information.
